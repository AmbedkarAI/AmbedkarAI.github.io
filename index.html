01 — About the Initiative

AI is a Tool for Justice.

The Ambedkar AI Initiative exists to place the power of Artificial Intelligence in the hands of communities historically denied access to knowledge. We translate the most consequential technology of our era into a language the people can hold, question, and wield.

Inspired by Dr. B. R. Ambedkar's vision of epistemic equity, we believe that liberation in the digital age demands more than legal equality — it demands technological and intellectual sovereignty for every oppressed community.

Foundation Track

03 — Research & Focus

Research & Focus

R — 01

Algorithmic Bias & Fair Representation

Auditing how AI systems encode caste, class, and social prejudice — and building methods that surface and correct them.

R — 02

AI Literacy for Social Movements

Translating technical literacy into organizing power, so movements can use AI as a tool for mobilization and accountability.

R — 03

Ethical AI Governance

Frameworks for accountable, transparent AI that centres the rights and dignity of historically marginalized people.

R — 04

Community-Driven Data Collection

Building datasets owned and governed by communities themselves, resisting extractive and exclusionary data regimes.

R — 05

AI for Economic Upliftment

Researching how AI tools and skills translate into livelihood, entrepreneurship, and social mobility for the oppressed.

R — 06

Open Source & Knowledge Commons

Championing free, open, and shared knowledge so that no community is locked out of the tools shaping their future.
